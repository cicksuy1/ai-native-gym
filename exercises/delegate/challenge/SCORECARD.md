# Scorecard — Module 7 (`delegate`)

The coach grades **how you delegated**, not the finding itself. Five dimensions, each `solid` /
`partial` / `missing`. Pass bar: every dimension *attempted*, **≥ 3/5 solid**, plus a cold-recall
answer. Weak dimensions are logged as "keep drilling".

Emphasis this module: **Delegated & isolated well** (the whole point).

| # | Dimension | What `solid` looks like *here* |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Planned before acting** | Made the delegate-vs-inline call deliberately (destination vs. journey; size vs. ~4× overhead). |
| 2 | **Engineered context** | Delegation *protected* the main window — the side-task's mess stayed in the subagent's context. |
| 3 | **Delegated & isolated well** ⭐ | Wrote a complete four-part brief (objective / format / tools / boundaries); the return was a scoped **summary**, not a transcript. |
| 4 | **Closed a verify loop** | Checked the subagent's summary was actually usable/correct, not taken on faith. |
| 5 | **Reviewed & stayed the executor** | Made the real decision yourself from the summary; didn't rubber-stamp the subagent's conclusion. |
