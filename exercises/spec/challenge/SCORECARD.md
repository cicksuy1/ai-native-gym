# Scorecard — Module 3 (`spec`)

The coach grades **how you turned a wish into a contract and drove it**, not whether the feature
merely works. Five dimensions, each `solid` / `partial` / `missing`. Pass bar: every dimension
*attempted*, **≥ 3/5 solid**, plus a cold-recall answer. Weak dimensions are logged as "keep
drilling" — they never block the pass.

Emphasis this module: **Planned before acting** (the spec is matured planning) and **Engineered
context** (the fresh-session handoff).

| # | Dimension | What `solid` looks like *here* |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Planned before acting** ⭐ | Interviewed to surface implicit requirements; wrote a self-contained `SPEC.md` with graduated boundaries; the spec did the senior thinking up front. |
| 2 | **Engineered context** ⭐ | Handed off in a *fresh session* (`/clear`); implemented one focused section at a time rather than dumping the whole spec. |
| 3 | **Delegated & isolated well** | `attempted` = recognized whether multiple agents could implement spec sections in parallel, and reasoned about it. |
| 4 | **Closed a verify loop** | The spec named a concrete check; you ran it and confirmed the result. |
| 5 | **Reviewed & stayed the executor** | Reviewed each section *against the contract*; caught drift by pointing at the spec, not memory. |
