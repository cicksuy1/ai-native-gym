# Scorecard — Module 12 (`orchestrate`) — capstone

The coach grades **your judgment across the whole course**, applied at scale. Five dimensions, each
`solid` / `partial` / `missing`. Pass bar: every dimension *attempted*, **≥ 3/5 solid**, plus a
cold-recall answer. As the capstone, **dimension 4 (Closed a verify loop) must be solid to pass** —
not just attempted. Weak dimensions are logged as "keep drilling".

This is the capstone — the emphasis is on the **orchestrate-vs-not judgment** (under *Planned before
acting*) and a real **verify** step. Knowing when *not* to orchestrate scores as highly as
orchestrating well.

| # | Dimension | What `solid` looks like *here* |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Planned before acting** ⭐ | Diagnosed scale + coupling; made the orchestrate-vs-single-session call correctly; scoped/tested the prompt before any fan-out. |
| 2 | **Engineered context** | "The script holds the loop" — your own context saw only the synthesis, not every agent's mess. |
| 3 | **Delegated & isolated well** | Fanned-out agents were scoped and isolated; coupled work was *not* force-split. |
| 4 | **Closed a verify loop** ⭐ | Used a verify pattern (adversarial verify / judge panel) or verified the result; didn't trust raw fan-out output. |
| 5 | **Reviewed & stayed the executor** | Owned the final synthesis/review; checkpointed; budgeted for the ~15× cost consciously. |
